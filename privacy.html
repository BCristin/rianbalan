Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. Who I am

This policy explains how Rian Balan, photographer (“I”, “me”), collects and uses your personal data when you use www.rianbalan.com (the “Website”). I am responsible for your personal data.

Contact: rianbalanph@gmail.com

2. What data I collect

Data you send me. When you use the contact form, I receive:

  • your name;
  • your email address and/or phone number;
  • the package you are interested in and your preferred date;
  • anything you write in your message.

Data sent automatically. When you visit the Website, your browser automatically shares technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, the page you asked for and the time of your visit. The hosting provider uses this to deliver the Website and keep it secure.

I do not ask for and do not want sensitive data, such as health information. Please do not include it in your message.

3. Why I use your data

  • to reply to your enquiry and talk about a session or a collaboration;
  • to arrange, deliver and keep records of a session you book with me;
  • to keep the Website working and secure.

I do not sell your data, and I do not use it for advertising.

Under UK data protection law, I use your data because you asked me to take steps before a possible booking, to carry out a booking, to meet my legal obligations (for example, keeping accounting records), or because I have a legitimate interest in running and protecting the Website.

4. Who else handles your data

I use a few trusted services to run the Website. They only receive the data they need to provide their service:

  • Vercel hosts the Website and processes the technical data described above.
  • FormSubmit delivers the contact form to my email.
  • Google provides my email inbox (Gmail), where your messages are stored, and the fonts used on the Website (Google Fonts), which means your browser connects to Google’s servers.
  • jsDelivr delivers the calendar used in the contact form.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. When that happens, they use safeguards required by data protection law.

I may also share your data if the law requires it, or to protect my rights if these terms are broken.

5. How long I keep your data

I keep enquiry messages for as long as needed to answer them and to follow up on a possible booking. If you book a session, I keep the details for as long as needed to deliver it and to meet legal and accounting obligations. After that, I delete them.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data I hold about you;
  • ask me to correct or update it;
  • ask me to delete it;
  • object to, or ask me to limit, how I use it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where I rely on it.

To use any of these rights, email rianbalanph@gmail.com. I will reply within one month.

If you are not happy with how I handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

7. Cookies

The Website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. When you close the cookie notice, your browser remembers it, so the notice does not appear again. This choice is stored only on your device.

The services listed in section 4 may use technical data to deliver their service, as described in their own privacy policies.

8. How I protect your data

I take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. The Website uses a secure (HTTPS) connection.

9. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The new version applies from the moment it is published on this page, and the date at the top shows when it was last changed.

10. Questions

For any questions about this policy or your data, email rianbalanph@gmail.com.
