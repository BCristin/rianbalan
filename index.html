Questions

How much does a photoshoot in London cost?

Your first session is at the standard price: Mini Story £235, Classic Story £285 and Signature Story £405. From your second session on, you pay the founding price: £195, £235 and £325.

What kind of photography do you offer?

Portrait photography, couple photography (love stories, engagements and anniversaries) and personal brand photography for creators and professionals. I also collaborate with creators, influencers and brands on visual projects.

I have never done a photoshoot. Is that a problem?

No. Every package includes posing guidance. I tell you where to stand and how to move, so you can relax and be yourself.

Where in London do the sessions take place?

We choose the location together before the session, based on the look you want. Signature Story includes two locations, or a studio and an outdoor location.

How many photos will I receive?

Mini Story includes 8 edited photos, Classic Story 20 and Signature Story 35–40. I edit each photo by hand and send them in an online gallery.

How do I book a session?

Send a request through the form below, by email at rianbalanph@gmail.com or on Instagram at @rianbalanph. Your session is booked once I confirm the date, package and price in writing.
