How it works

  1. Choose a package

    Send me a message with the package, the date you have in mind and what the photos are for.

  2. Plan together

    We agree on the location and outfits. For Signature Story, I prepare a moodboard.

  3. The session

    I tell you where to stand and how to move. You just have to be yourselves.

  4. Your photos

    I pick the best frames and edit each one by hand, then send them in an online gallery.
